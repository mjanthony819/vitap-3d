About this map

An unofficial 3D map of the VIT-AP University campus in Amaravati, built by a student for fun and orientation. Not affiliated with or endorsed by VIT-AP University.

  • Building shapes, roads, paths and places come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).
  • Heights are approximate except where mappers recorded floors (CB 11, AB-1/AB-2 3). Blocks are simple extrusions, not architectural models.
  • Walking routes follow mapped paths only and assume 5 km/h. Missing or fenced shortcuts are not shown.
  • Sun position is computed from real IST time; windows and lamps light up after dark.
  • Some campus buildings are not yet mapped in OSM, so they may be missing or unnamed here.
